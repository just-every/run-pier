# Business documents and invoices

In **Sales orders → Customer invoices and credits**, choose the order's invoicing policy before preparing its invoice. Shipment invoices use that shipment's quantities; prepay invoices use the accepted order before dispatch. Review the exact draft, then authorize its immutable original. Invoice dates, due dates, charges, tax-table evidence and any reviewed CAD rate stay with that version. Credit notes name an original invoice line or freight charge. A paid invoice needs a credit rather than a void. For CAD conversion, select the order, enter each reviewed CAD base-unit price and the conversion reason, and retain the USD value of one CAD. An unpaid invoice can be voided with a reason after its active checkout has been expired.

Native invoices and credits show the confirmed QuickBooks posting state for the mapped, tax-exempt USD subset; unsupported documents and provider-tax posting stay pending. Enabling native invoicing stops the legacy automatic shipment-invoice proposals for that order, so they cannot duplicate the native document. Existing attempted legacy postings require reconciliation before native invoicing; stock and COGS remain separate. Taxable QuickBooks posting and Avalara acceptance remain pending.

Under **Business documents and templates**, select a document kind and its real source record. **Generate exact PDF** retains the source snapshot, template version and exact PDF bytes. **Open exact PDF** always reprints those bytes. To use changed company details, terms or templates, generate a new document deliberately. Templates are grouped into sales, purchasing and inventory, with separate email subject/body fields. Supported merge fields are listed on the template form. Quoted prices, customer/supplier item aliases, commercial packs, base units and available trace identities are rendered from their sources.

Add the company name and address in **Purchasing** first. The renderer embeds Noto Sans and the current Pier mark, or an owner-supplied PNG logo through `print.template.save`. Unsupported glyphs are refused explicitly; no character is silently discarded. In the pack station, **Generate retained packing PDF** records only the cartons actually scanned into that pack, alongside the existing current-pack browser slip. Generating or printing never dispatches stock.

**Email a retained business PDF** requires explicit owner consent to its recipient. The record keeps the same PDF, recipient, approval time and provider outcome. Sent means provider acceptance. A retained quote can be emailed only while its exact quote version and live approval authority remain current; earlier PDFs stay downloadable. Failed, unavailable and uncertain submissions stay visible; an uncertain request is never blindly resent. Configure the sending provider's signed delivery endpoint at `/api/pier/print/delivery-events` under **Signed delivery notifications**. A verified bounce changes the recorded send to failed and never triggers another send.

The straight bill of lading uses the VICS form structure with owner-entered shipper/consignee, carrier SCAC/PRO, freight terms, handling units, packages, net/gross weights and NMFC classification. The physical pickup signatures remain blank. Long source particulars continue on labeled supplements.

## Invoice-specific payment links

Connect the matching Stripe test or live account under **Invoice payment connection**, with explicit owner consent and its webhook signing secret. Configure `/api/pier/payments/stripe/webhook` in that account. **Create invoice payment link** creates a signed link to that invoice only. Generate the invoice again to include its link; an emailed statement includes links to its open invoices. The Stripe adapter pins its protocol version and filters Dashboard-enabled payment methods to card and, for USD, US bank account. Card and US bank-account details are entered on Stripe's hosted page, never in Pier.

Bank verification and processing are not paid. If Stripe requires microdeposit verification, the invoice page links to that invoice’s Stripe verification page and accepts no second checkout while verification is pending. Pier applies an amount only after readback confirms the exact Stripe invoice identity, currency, amount and successful payment. Replayed events/readbacks cannot duplicate it. The page then shows paid and accepts no further payment. A credit or void expires the old link. An open or uncertain checkout must be reconciled and explicitly expired before changing its invoice; processing bank payments must finish first. If a creation response is lost, find its existing Checkout Session using the retained idempotency key in Stripe, then **Check Stripe payment**. Pier does not recreate that uncertain request.

The full payment, ledger, refund, dispute and settlement obligations remain PAY-001, SALES-006 and ACCOUNT-001/002.
