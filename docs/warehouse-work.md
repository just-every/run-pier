# Receiving, picking and packing

Receive a committed purchase order from **Floor → Receive**. Choose the put-away bin, scan unit or case codes, and post the delivery reference after reviewing the tally. A partial delivery leaves the remaining purchase quantity expected. Tracked goods retain their lot, serial and expiry through the same work; see [Traceability](traceability.md). Transfers and Counts retain their physical handoff and reviewed movement frontier.

On **Floor → Pick**, assign orders to a carrier pickup and dock, then choose a single bin walk or zone picks. Every order has a numbered tote. A batch walk follows bin order while retaining the reservation for each order; a shortage cannot take another order's allocation. Scan the required bin before its item. **Bin empty or short** directs only that line to fitting unreserved overflow stock, opens a count for the reported bin, and leaves an unfilled remainder on its original order.

The owner assigns bin picking zones in the **Picking zones** panel. Zone waves retain those assignments when released. Each team selects its zone and picks into the order's numbered tote. Pack shows each order's zone contributions; it cannot close that order's pack while one of those contributions is still outstanding. Other completed orders may pack independently. Closing the pack retains the merged zones, operator and time. Changing a bin's zone later applies to future waves.

On **Floor → Pack**, scan each picked unit into its carton, start another carton when needed, and record each carton's weight and dimensions. **Print packing slip** opens the current pack's exact carton contents, order, tote, wave, dock and recorded picked bins. Tracked contents name their retained identities and kits show their components. Outstanding demand and earlier shipments are excluded. Printing and marking packed do not ship stock; the physical dispatch confirmation does. A short order's remaining quantity can join a later wave and shipment.

Interrupted floor work stays in the same browser tab, including the selected task, confirmed bin and exact interrupted command. Network loss is visible. Reconnecting or reloading sends no queued write automatically. Review the current records and use **Retry this exact action** to recover a recorded outcome or submit the same still-current intent. Changed tasks or availability require a fresh physical review and scan. Retained scans pause for review after an interruption; uncertain writes must be recovered before their scan can be discarded. A definite wrong-bin or wrong-item refusal can be corrected without pretending that a stock movement occurred.

At **Floor → Returns**, scan the destination bin and record the inspection decision, then scan the returned unit. An owner or warehouse inspector can restock an intact unit in that one intake action, or write off a damaged unit against the owner's mapped Books expense account. An operator without inspection authority receives either condition into quarantine for a separate inspection. Ambiguous dispatches require an explicit source choice. Credits, valuation and write-off jobs remain visible in Books and reconcile through the connected ledger.

Physical scanner, camera and printer acceptance remains recorded separately under SCAN-001/002; carrier postage and provider labels remain SHIP-001 work. Printable business templates remain PRINT-001 work. These boundaries do not turn a scan, print or postage purchase into dispatch.
