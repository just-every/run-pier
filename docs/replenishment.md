# Replenishment and customer dates

Open Purchasing → Demand and replenishment → Review policy. The 30/90/365-day and chosen-window sales rates come from actual Pier shipments, linked to their source documents. Lead-time demand plus your safety stock proposes a reorder point; the review period proposes a quantity. Save the supplier, lead time, safety stock and windows, then apply the proposed points and quantities. Applying them requires a buyer and current inputs. New sales never silently change your applied parameters.

A fixed-quantity rule adds its quantity and point, then subtracts the position. An order-up-to rule subtracts the position from its maximum. The position includes sellable stock minus reserved and backordered demand, plus committed purchase remainders and inbound transfers. Quarantine and forecasts do not count. Suggestions round to supplier packs and minimum quantities. Existing drafts reduce the remaining suggestion, so repeated planning does not buy the same shortage twice. The list shows each calculation term, expected freight/duty and landed cost. Automatic drafts prepare purchases within the saved item/warehouse policy; a buyer still approves each exact PO. Turning off automatic drafts pauses that policy.

Review the PO, approve and commit its exact version, then review the recipient under Send purchase order to supplier. The approval covers the PO contents, expected cost and at most five confirmation chases spaced 1–30 days apart. Every minute the scheduler checks due approved messages and low-stock policies. Chases require provider acceptance of the initial send and stop when confirmed, fully received, materially changed or paused. Use Stop supplier chases to revoke them. Read approved messages and delivery outcomes on the record. A provider acceptance means submitted; it does not establish inbox delivery. Failed or uncertain submissions are not retried blindly. Inspect the provider before authorizing a new message.

To delegate the initial send, commit the buyer-approved PO and open **Let the inventory agent send this PO**. Approve its exact recipient, landed USD spend ceiling, expiry and reminder bounds. The scheduled inventory agent sends it once; an authenticated agent can also run `po.sendApproved` with only the PO identity/version. It cannot choose another recipient, change the PO, approve a purchase or exceed the ceiling. **Stop delegated send and chases** revokes pending work. Changes to demand, supplier terms, replenishment policy or business destination invalidate the authority and require renewed buyer review. Expiry stops an initial send; already authorized reminder intervals stay bounded. The record retains the approving human, executor, message and provider outcome. An unavailable provider may be configured later, but failed or uncertain submissions require reconciliation and are never blindly retried.

Record the supplier’s actual reply and message reference on the PO. Approving a confirmed date keeps that reply, updates the PO and recomputes affected customer dates. Incoming mail is recorded by an authorized person in this round; mailbox polling and automatic extraction are not implemented.

Sales orders show backordered quantities, the POs supplying them and whether their date is confirmed or an estimate. Supply quantities are consumed once in customer-promise/order-date priority. Without enough confirmed supply, a supplier lead-time estimate is clearly labeled. Review the computed date and customer recipient to send a re-promise, or authorize Future date updates for that exact order and recipient. Later confirmed PO date changes re-promise and email automatically within that authorization. Disable the policy to stop future automatic updates. Customer promise history and every approved message remain visible.

Business email uses the install’s configured `MAIL_PROVIDER` (`resend`, `sendgrid` or `cloudflare`), `MAIL_FROM`, `MAIL_API_KEY` and, for Cloudflare, `MAIL_ACCOUNT_ID`. Authentication keeps its existing local sign-in behavior. Missing business mail configuration and RER Managed installations show one line: **Email is not available yet.** No provider call is attempted on Managed. Fix configuration and use Process due approved emails to submit previously unavailable messages; ambiguous submissions remain unresolved. Restored installs do not acquire approval authority from retained identities: establish fresh native access, review policies and reconnect deliberately.

Forecasts currently use native shipments. Imported/channel history, seasonality, promotions, stockout correction, service-level optimization, company-wide rules, cash limits and accounting closure remain named target gaps in CONTRACT.md. Dates and forecasts do not create stock or guarantee a physical delivery.
