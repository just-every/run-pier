# Transfers and counts

Stock moves between warehouses through a transit location you can see, and counts check a bin against the movements Pier recorded for it.

## Move stock between warehouses

In **Transfers**, **Dispatch available stock to transit** moves stock from a source bin into a transit location on its way to a destination bin. Stock in transit is not available. When some stock arrives, enter the physically received quantity in **Receive transfer** and choose **Record destination receipt**. Received units enter the destination bin; the remainder stays in transit and unavailable for sale. **Confirm full destination receipt** receives only the remainder. Repeating an interrupted action recovers its original result without moving stock twice.

## Count a bin

In **Counts**, choose the bin in **Capture bin count frontier** and **Begin count**. Pier remembers exactly which movements the count covers. Enter what is on the shelf in **Record physical quantities**, with the evidence or reason, and **Save physical count for review**. A person then approves the exact adjustment (**Approve version … adjustment**), or chooses **Capture a recount**. If stock moved after the capture, Pier asks for a recount rather than overwrite that movement.
