# Lot, serial and expiry traceability

Open an item's Stock details and use **Lot and serial traceability** to configure tracking before receiving its first stock. The owner approves lot or unique-serial tracking, FIFO or FEFO allocation, and whether expiry is required. An established tracking mode does not reinterpret old history; an existing untracked SKU needs a reviewed identity cutover rather than guessed serials or lots. Item metadata edits do not remove tracking.

Receive against a committed purchase order on Floor → Receive. A GS1 scan can fill GTIN (01), supplier lot (10), expiry (17), production date (11) and serial (21). Parenthesized element strings and scanner-decoded FNC1-separated strings are supported. For ordinary item barcodes, expand **Enter lot, serial and expiry by hand**. Serialized units are received and moved whole, with one unique serial for each unit. A replay of the same command recovers its original result; a new receipt cannot reuse a retained serial.

Supplier lot references identify lots within a SKU. Each retained identity also has its own unique Pier scan code; serials and those allocated codes share the existing stock-code namespace. Serial lookup and uniqueness are case-insensitive; supplier lot references retain their exact text. Repeated receipts of one lot keep its original expiry and production date. The expiry date is valid through that UTC calendar day; an expired receipt, pick or dispatch is refused. GS1 day `00` means the last day of the encoded month.

FIFO follows the identity's first received time and its retained arrival sequence within a receipt. FEFO sorts by expiry first, with undated stock last, then that FIFO order. Allocation considers identities across warehouse bins before choosing bins. Transfers and partial dispatches retain the exact identities and original cost layers. Reserved sources on Sales show their lot/serial and expiry. Scan the allocated lot/serial when picking and packing; a different lot, an expired identity or another order's identity cannot be substituted silently.

Select an identity in Stock to inspect source receipts, suppliers, affected shipments and customers, returns and retained movement records. A fully shipped identity remains selectable. Warehouse staff can quarantine a supplier lot with a reason; its current sellable stock moves to quarantine and affected reservations are released as whole order lines or kits. Quarantine follows retained parent identities into assembled kit lots. Protected owner-controlled channel holds must be resolved by their owner before a conflicting physical movement can post. Ordinary traceability does not certify regulated release or recall compliance.

To return a tracked unit, select its actual dispatch and line, destination bin, quantity and reason in the trace panel. The return preserves the dispatched identity in quarantine. The existing Sales quarantine review controls restocking. Returned serials use their actual shipment; another receipt is not a return.

Tracked components can be sold in virtual kits. To pre-assemble them, configure tracking on the kit SKU and retain its reviewed output lot/serial on the kitting task. Its expiry cannot outlive its earliest-expiring tracked component. Disassembly recovers the retained component identities and costs. A virtual kit returned as an assembled bundle receives a new quarantined kit identity while retaining its dispatched component lineage and expiry.

Stock totals sum the actual eligible bin availability. Expired or blocked goods retain their physical movement and reservation history, with the excluded quantity shown beside their stock status. The full business JSON export and complete installation archive retain identities, trace events, allocation records, policies and source links. The older native CSV cutover shape refuses stocked tracked goods instead of dropping their identities.
