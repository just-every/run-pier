# Contract mechanism notes

These notes hold the mechanism, constants and accounting rules that the contract clauses link to. They were moved out of `CONTRACT.md` in the 28 October 2026 contract cleanup; the clause states the requirement and these notes state how Pier meets it today.

## CAT-003

The owner-visible refusal for an unsupported subject erasure reads as follows. An unsupported request visibly refuses with: “Erasure refused: subject attribution and governed erasure for arbitrary custom-field content and retained copies are not configured. Owner setup is required before Pier can identify and erase this subject’s content. No content was deleted by this request.”

## WMS-002

Current location policies measure usable volume in cubic centimetres and axis-aligned length/width/height in centimetres; inch item dimensions convert exactly at 2.54 cm per inch. Capacity includes sellable and quarantined base-unit quantities across all items. Permitted-item lists are explicit (an empty list permits every dimensioned item); unknown dimensions, an oversized item or excess occupied volume refuse placement atomically through receiving, transfer, count and import doors. Unconfigured bins keep their established behavior. Pick faces replenish when unreserved sellable quantity is at or below the minimum, toward the maximum; only unreserved reserve stock and remaining destination capacity are proposed. Empty faces have first priority, other low faces second and optional receiving-bin put-away third; shorter aisle/bay Manhattan walking distance orders equal-priority tasks and pick sequence breaks travel ties. Aisle and bay are non-negative metre coordinates measured from the warehouse entrance.

ABC evidence counts confirmed pick scans during the preceding 30 days, grouped by SKU, with scans/day truncated to six decimals. Descending scan count (SKU breaks ties) assigns A/B/C by the cumulative share before that SKU, using 80% and 95% boundaries; no observations means unranked, not an invented rating. Recommendations name the closest fitting permitted pick face by entrance distance, then observed mean elapsed time from wave release to confirmed pick, then pick sequence. Those retained wave/line observations are elapsed task times, not isolated walking measurements. Item dimensions, capacity and travel evidence remain inspectable. Recommendations alone change no policy or stock. Until a supervisor role exists, the owner approves policies and exact plans; warehouse staff confirm physical movement with source-bin, destination-bin and item scans, the exact approved base quantity and device. Changed bin/item definitions, revoked approval authority, unavailable reserved stock or a newly full destination refuse without partial effects. Every completed task retains its approval and confirmation in business export, preserves cost layers and cannot replay a movement. KIT-001 and TRACE-001 must extend permitted-stock, capacity and task identity for their new stock kinds before shipping.

## BUY-001

Implementation boundary: current native channel observations use the first Pier capture time, not a provider sale date. Mapped orders are counted net of native cancellations at their routed warehouse, or the connection warehouse when no route is set. Their linked shipments contribute no second observation. This is native captured demand, not historical provider backfill; MIGRATE-002 and CHANNEL-001 retain their history obligations.

## BUY-004

Current supplier scorecards are available through authenticated `GET /api/pier/supplier-performance`. They group by supplier and SKU/base unit, never total unlike units. Fill is gross unreversed posted receipt quantity divided by current committed PO quantity (including open commitments); over-receipts are visible as excess and can exceed 100% fill. On-time is received quantity on or before the supplier-confirmed date, divided by receipt quantity with a retained confirmation, comparing inclusive UTC calendar days. Both receiving doors snapshot the approved confirmation and supplier reply at posting; later confirmations do not rewrite earlier receipts. Missing or legacy confirmation evidence is unknown and excluded only from the on-time denominator; reversed receipts are excluded from every quantity but remain cited. Percentages truncate to six decimals, with a null zero-denominator rate. Receipt quantities, cost-versus-PO unit-price differences, source IDs and count-correction links remain inspectable. Stock corrections and customer returns do not establish supplier defects. Defects come only from supplier inspections: `receipt.inspect` (warehouse, buyer or owner) appends a finding to a posted receipt line with its inspected quantity, defective quantity and a required defect description when any are defective, capped at the quantity received on that line; it moves no stock and changes no cost. A scorecard item shows inspected, defective and uninspected quantity and `defectPercent`, defective divided by inspected and truncated to six decimals, null when nothing is inspected; uninspected goods are not assumed good or defective, and a reversed receipt keeps its cited inspections but leaves every rate.

Current invoice/BOL source matching is an operator transcription check, not provider verification or automated extraction (DOC-001). Optional invoice and BOL text retains the submitted text (up to 2,000 characters per source), references and selected PO lines. Source quantity multiplied by base units per pack must be exact to six decimals and equal the billed base quantity. The source base unit must equal the item unit; a pack other than one must equal the committed supplier terms pack, or the immutable item pack when no terms exist. Source-line differences cannot be excused by quantity/price tolerances; those continue to apply to receipt and price differences only. Revisions retain prior source text and transcriptions; approval freezes the source and posting attachment.

Freight and tax are matched, not inferred. Each supplier invoice records freight and tax as non-negative USD amounts in whole cents (default 0). The retained invoice transcription states both (none shown is 0) and must equal them; a BOL that states freight must equal the invoice freight, while a BOL that states none is unknown, not zero. Neither comparison is excused by a tolerance. Against the PO, cumulative approved invoice freight and tax (earlier approved invoices plus this one) may not exceed the PO's expected `freight` and supplier `tax` (default 0). An excess is a named difference that fits tolerance only when it is at most the invoice's price tolerance percent of the PO amount (a zero PO amount tolerates none); a lower amount is no difference, because a later invoice may carry the rest. Receipts record landed freight and duty separately, from carrier and customs bills, and invoice freight is not compared to it, so freight is never counted twice: approved invoice freight posts as a line of the Pantry bill to the Freight and duty account, from which Pier's receipt journal moves receipt-landed freight into Inventory, and approved invoice tax posts to the chosen Purchase tax account, never to Inventory. Pier does not decide whether that tax is recoverable. An invoice carrying freight or tax with no chosen account waits in review instead of posting a partial payable. Credits, refunds, foreign currency and supplier-side tax calculation are outside this clause; a credit memo is BUY-003.

## BUY-005

Current planning covers the stocked item and demand types that exist today. KIT-001 and every later source or record capability must extend the same position, list, report and exception definition before shipping. A location with no override inherits the SKU default. Company mode sums the included locations’ configured reorder points (a location with neither a point nor a SKU default contributes zero); an explicit SKU company point overrides that sum. Company purchasing uses the SKU default rule and supplier, otherwise the default receiving warehouse’s rule, otherwise the first configured warehouse in the owner’s included list. Unassigned legacy PO supply stays in STOCK-001’s separate unassigned bucket and does not reduce any warehouse reorder need, including the company sum of included warehouses. A supplier switch uses that supplier’s case pack, minimum, optional reorder quantity, price and lead time. The grouped draft’s minimum value is the largest required minimum among its selected supplier-item terms and is flagged rather than padded. The current low-stock report follows today’s planning scope even when other report sections use a historical valuation date. Draft deletion restores suggestions immediately; an enabled automatic policy can prepare them again on later authorized work or its schedule.

## API-001

The supported incremental stock source uses a durable total order of absolute physical position events and actor-scoped page tokens. Fetching never advances a consumer: only explicit acknowledgement after processing advances its checkpoint; interruption replays the outstanding page with stable event IDs. Current role and warehouse grants fence every read and acknowledgement, and changed grants or stale or foreign tokens refuse resume. Pages contain at most 100 minimal physical events, with observation time and event-age lag; no cost, pricing or customer data is exposed. API-002, AUTO-001, SYNC-001, ADMIN-001, SEC-002 and DATA-001 and DATA-002 inherit these source, authority and retained-checkpoint semantics when they extend this door. This states the shipped semantic without adding an acceptance point.

## ADMIN-001

Implemented role semantics: Purchasing is the existing buyer role; Sales uses the existing sales-operation command authority, while Finance receives physical reads only until wider financial delegation is ruled. Owner-set cost and price switches only remove role authority. A physical grant permits admitted operational receiving, picking, packing, dispatch and freshly projected physical slips without costs, prices, customer identity or arbitrary fields. Raw supplier notices, retained commercial documents, financial files and installation-wide sends require wider authority. Saved results require the original person and the same current role revision; changing visibility also revokes dependent approval and send authority.
